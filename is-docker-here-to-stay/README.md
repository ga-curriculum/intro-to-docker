<h1>
  <span class="headline">Intro to Docker</span>
  <span class="subhead">Debate: Is Docker Here to Stay?</span>
</h1>

**Learning objective:** By the end of this lesson, students will be able to evaluate the advantages and limitations of Docker and containerization, and present well-supported arguments on whether container-based architecture principles have long-term viability in software development.

Docker and containerization have taken the developer world by storm, but is it a lasting trend, or could it fade with the arrival of new technologies? Let’s break down this question together in a debate!

<div class="activity discussion">
  <h2 class="title">Debate and Defend: Is Docker Here to Stay?</h2>
  <span class="minutes">20</span>
</div>

Let's divide the room into two groups: **Pro Docker** and **No Docker**.

Each group will research their position and make a case, using relevant examples to support their arguments.

## Instructions

**Step 1**: In your assigned groups, spend the next 5–10 minutes researching your stance. Use the prompts below to help structure your arguments.

**Step 2**: After researching, we’ll start the Great Docker Debate! Each side will present their case and respond to questions from the other group.

## Pro Docker

Consider the following points to support your argument:

- Benefits of Docker and Containerization: What unique advantages does Docker bring to development workflows? How does it impact resource efficiency, speed of deployment, or scalability?

- Long-Term Value of Container-Based Architecture: How might the core principles of Docker (like isolation, portability, and scalability) influence software development in the future? Do these principles have historical or theoretical support?

- Examples of Success with Docker: What types of businesses or applications are especially well-suited to Docker? Highlight examples where Docker has enabled transformation (ex: microservices, CI/CD pipelines) or helped solve specific industry challenges.

- Docker in Emerging Tech: Is Docker adaptable to new tech trends, such as serverless computing, cloud-native apps, or machine learning? How might containerization contribute to future tech growth?

## No Docker

Consider the following points to support your argument:

- Drawbacks and Limitations of Docker: What challenges or trade-offs come with using Docker? Are there scalability, performance, or security issues that make it unsuitable for some use cases?

- Alternatives to Containerization: What alternatives are available (ex: traditional VMs, serverless architectures) that might be more suitable for specific use cases? How might new tech innovations replace Docker in the near future?

- Examples of Companies Moving Away from Docker: Are there cases where companies found Docker to be unsuitable or transitioned away from it? Consider examples where Docker’s complexity, overhead, or incompatibility might have been an issue.

- Sustainability of Containerization Trends: Is containerization truly aligned with long-term software engineering goals, or might it be a temporary solution? What factors (like cost, emerging architectures) might limit Docker’s growth or lead to alternatives?
